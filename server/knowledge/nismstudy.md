# NISMSTUDY Knowledge Base

## Brand And Scope

NISMSTUDY is a learning website for candidates preparing for NISM, NCFM, and financial market certification exams. It offers curated question banks, realistic mock tests, and free exam workbooks.

NISMSTUDY is not an official NISM, SEBI, NSE, NSE Academy, BSE, or regulatory website. It is an independent exam-preparation platform.

## Pricing And Access

The standard price shown on the website is Rs 329 per exam.

Paid mock-test access is valid for 15 days after access is granted.

Each exam course is intended to include 400-500 curated questions with explanations and unlimited mock attempts during the access period.

## Courses And Study Material

NISMSTUDY publishes exam-specific courses for NISM and NCFM preparation. Students should choose the course that matches the certification exam they are preparing for.

The website offers free PDF workbooks through the Free Materials page. Workbooks help with theory, while mock tests help with exam-style practice.

## Login And Account Access

The website uses email magic-link login. Students sign in with their email address and receive a secure login link.

For account-specific login, payment, course access, or mock-test issues, users should email info@nismstudy.in.

## Mock Tests

Mock tests are designed to simulate exam-style practice. Users can attempt mock tests repeatedly during their access period.

Students should review explanations after each attempt and repeat weak areas before the exam.
