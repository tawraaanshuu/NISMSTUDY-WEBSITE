Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about our practice exams, pricing and the official NISM exam process.

About our practice exams

What is the cost of the practice exams?

A flat Rs 329 for every exam and duration — including NISM X-A, X-B, PMS, AIF and all other series.

Do I receive a book or a soft copy of the practice questions?

No printed material is shipped — all practice questions are online. Once payment is complete your account is activated immediately and you can log in to practise.

What is the validity period of the practice exams?

Access is valid for 15 days from activation with unlimited attempts during that window.

How many questions do I get to practise?

Each exam includes the most important 400–500 practice questions with answers, updated regularly so you learn exactly what's needed to pass in the first attempt.

How do I make payment?

Login, choose your exam and click Buy Access. You'll be taken to a secure payment page supporting UPI, cards and net banking. Access activates immediately after successful payment.

How do I see my purchased exams?

Login with your email and open the Dashboard — all your purchased exams and remaining access days are shown there. Your active mocks are also listed under Mock Tests.

About the official NISM / NCFM exams

Who can join NCFM / NISM?

Anyone can join. No formal educational background or age requirement is necessary for registration.

How do I register with NISM?

Register at certifications.nism.ac.in. A PAN card and a digital photograph are compulsory for registration.

How much does the actual NISM / NCFM exam cost?

Official exam fees generally range from Rs 1,250 to Rs 3,000 depending on the module. (Our practice material for any module is Rs 329.)
